Enter Adonissimo starts the song at a quiet default volume with a short fade-in.
Enter without music opens the site silently. The music dock provides play/pause,
mute, and volume controls. The same audio element continues across client-side
project navigation. Hiding the browser tab does not pause the main song;
manual pause/mute remains authoritative. Browser/OS suspension may still apply.

To replace the recording, add another real audio file here and edit lib/music.ts.
MP3, OGG, M4A and WAV filename extensions are supported by the local source validator.
Actual codec support still depends on the visitor's browser.
