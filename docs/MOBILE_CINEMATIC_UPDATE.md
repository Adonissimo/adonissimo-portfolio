# Mobile and cinematic update — 3.9.0

The later [3.10.0 opening update](TRIFORCE_OPENING_UPDATE.md) supersedes this edition's static modal-cursor fallback and 620ms welcome exit, and adds fuller mist/mural panels. The phone layout and audio behavior below otherwise remain.

This source update combines the requested phone layout, animated fairy, moving environment atmosphere, and welcome-screen effects/audio. It does not deploy the site or complete the remaining Phase 4 live checks.

## Phone layout

- Compact navigation starts at 960px and includes Contact. It has 48px links, a viewport-bounded scrollable panel, Escape/outside/focus-departure closing and a no-JavaScript layout.
- Safe-area gutters accommodate notches and landscape orientation; viewport scaling remains available.
- Small project rows become readable single-column entries. Forms retain 16px controls; OAuth actions stack rather than shrinking their text. Tables and code blocks scroll within their own region.
- The dock keeps its minimized default, uses a narrow-screen control layout and 44px sliders, collapses when a phone user focuses a typing field, and returns focus to its toggle when closed with Escape. These actions do not pause the main music.
- The requested wide PNG footer remains, at 66px on phones. The theme PNG sources, local fonts and genuine loading states remain.

## Effects by place

The public ambient field mounts 32 particles on desktop and 18 on small/touch-first devices; quiet/private routes mount eight. Sky/fields use drifting leaves and motes, the workshop uses small Zonai flecks and the Depths use rising lightroot-like motes. Warm day rays become cooler moonlight at night; Zonai/Depths light is jade. Riverside mist is a little fuller; auth/admin/neutral pages have quieter lighting and mist. Layers sit behind content and do not intercept taps.

Mobile keeps visible text/image arrivals and the book/rune transition, with shorter timing and six magical sparks instead of twelve. It omits the scroll-driven background parallax and full-screen image scaling; small particles, mist and lighting still move. Hidden documents pause the ambient field. Ambient effects in the expanded dock still pauses decoration independently of music/navigation. This edition retains the owner's explicit full-motion preference.

On fine-pointer devices, the fairy has moving wing groups: a 750ms half-cycle at rest, accelerating with pointer speed down to a bounded 90ms. It remains aligned with the pointer and has a fixed 28-light trail. Text/form controls retain native cursors, and touch-only devices mount no fairy nodes. A native modal uses the visible static fairy cursor fallback because normal fixed layers render below the dialog. Blur/hidden/cleanup clears the sprite and trails; the static SVG also remains the fallback when animation is unsupported.

## Opening and audio

Three Triforce pieces assemble into the crest, with a rotating carved seal, sweeping gold rays, rising motes, falling leaves and two drifting mist layers. Enter releases one bright gold/ivory bloom during the existing 620ms exit; it is a single swell rather than a repeating flash. Enter, Enter silently, Skip and Escape remain usable immediately. The opening mounts 12 leaf/mote nodes on compact devices and 24 on desktop; decorative animation pauses when the document is hidden.

`public/audio/forest-prelude.wav` is an original 40-second synthetic wind/birds/soft-flute soundscape, generated by the included offline script. It is not a recording or melody from a Zelda game. The shipped WAV needs no runtime generation or extra package.

The forest player attempts playback as soon as the welcome mounts, with **no forest sound button**. Audible autoplay cannot be guaranteed: if the browser blocks it, a trusted pointer/key interaction with the opening retries. Entry/Skip controls are excluded from that retry so they cannot revive the forest audio during exit. A replay pauses any playing main track before the forest attempt. Enter pauses the forest first, then starts the supplied Zelda's Lullaby and opening cue in the same gesture. Enter silently pauses music and disables interaction effects; Skip does not start the main song. Preview audio has separate in-memory volume and never writes the main music preferences. Pending playback is invalidated on exit/detach.
