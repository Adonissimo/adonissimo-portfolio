## Elevation & Depth

Tonal layers and thin rules provide resting depth. Project entries have no resting shadow; inventories, articles, tables and contact use open rows or rules. Natural shading belongs to artwork and the cinematic book leaf, not to glass panels or a glow-based UI.

The actual shared primary hover shadow is a small lift (`0 3px 12px #0003`). Body theme colors transition over (250ms). Full-motion controls use short feedback, including a gold sweep, a slight primary-button lift, drawn click ripple and bounded illustration zoom. Reduced motion and the manual motion preference disable animation/transitions and smooth scrolling. Loading skeletons are static in every motion mode; a readable status communicates their purpose without a repeating animation.

**The Tonal First Rule.** Use surfaces and rules for ordinary structure; reserve cast depth and spatial movement for purposeful interaction or the book signature.
